The buyer

The VP of Enterprise Architecture has a board commitment to keep.

The TIBCO BW renewal lands in nine months. The PowerCenter extended-support clock runs out in March 2027. Finance has explicitly said it will not fund another three-year time-and-materials migration. Every system integrator invited in has quoted "depends on complexity" — not one has produced a defensible artifact inventory the CIO can put in front of the audit committee.

Migrate is the answer. A two-to-three-week assessment produces the artifact inventory and the conversion risk register before any code moves. The full migration then runs as a fixed-scope sprint with a quoted week count and a named deliverable list. The CIO has board-ready evidence at every milestone. The VP keeps the commitment.
