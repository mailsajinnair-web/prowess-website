FAQ

Questions we get on the scoping call.

How does the AI Migration Agent compare with rewriting by hand?
The agent drafts the MuleSoft flow from the legacy source, the architect reviews and corrects, the developer ships. The agent reduces typing, not judgement. The 80% accuracy figure means the architect's editing time drops by roughly that proportion. It does not mean architects are out of the loop.
Will the agent handle our custom TIBCO BW6 adapters?
Standard adapters convert cleanly. Custom adapters require a one-time mapping pattern; we have a library of mapping patterns for the 30+ adapters we see most often in BW6 estates. New patterns are inside the fixed fee.
How do you handle parallel run?
Standard practice is two to four weeks of parallel run with the legacy platform live, reconciling message-by-message. Reconciliation reports are part of the deliverable.
CloudHub 2.0, Runtime Fabric, or on-prem?
All three are supported. The target topology is decided in Step 1 (Discovery), based on your data residency, latency, and existing infrastructure footprint.
What if we run on Mule 3 today?
Mule 3 to Mule 4 is a Migration Sprint MuleSoft engagement. We have a dedicated path for it including the DataWeave 1.0 to 2.0 conversion.
Who actually shows up?
The Principal Architect from your scoping call is the Principal Architect on the engagement. Their photo is on the architects page.
