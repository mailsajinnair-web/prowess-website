Why master data sits underneath AI

Master data is the foundation production AI runs on.

AI agents reason over whichever customer, account, or contract record the integration layer hands them. When the master-data layer reconciles those records cleanly, the agent answers correctly. When it does not, the use case stalls in pilot.

Informatica IDMC and MDM are the master-data and data-management layer that gives the agent a single, governed view. We migrate legacy ETL and master-data tooling onto IDMC and MDM as a fixed-fee Sprint so the data foundation is in place before the agent goes near production.

FAQ

Questions we get on the scoping call.

Why Informatica over Snowflake-native ETL or dbt?
Snowflake is the warehouse. dbt is transformation in the warehouse. Informatica IDMC is the integration and data-management layer above the warehouse, with MDM and Data Quality as services the warehouse does not provide natively. For enterprises that need master data, governance, and lineage across multiple consumers, IDMC plus MDM remains the cleanest pattern.
Do we have to use MuleSoft alongside IDMC?
No. The Informatica Sprint is platform-independent for the integration layer. MuleSoft and IDMC together is the most common pattern in our manufacturing book, but IDMC alone, IDMC plus Snowflake, and IDMC plus a Kafka backbone are all supported.
How do you handle data-quality remediation during migration?
The discovery phase produces a data-quality baseline. The design phase decides which DQ rules ship with the migration and which are sequenced after stabilization. We do not block migration on full DQ remediation; we baseline, deploy, then iterate.
Cloud-only or hybrid?
IDMC is cloud-native, but we support hybrid topologies where on-prem agents read source systems that cannot be exposed.
Who reviews the MDM domain model?
The Principal Architect plus a customer-side data steward. The model is reviewed before Step 3 begins, so implementation does not start against an unconfirmed domain design.
Will this slow our existing AI projects?
The opposite. The Sprint surfaces the master-data layer your AI projects already depend on. Most teams find one of their stalled agent pilots unblocks during Sprint Step 2.
