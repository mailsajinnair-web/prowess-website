The problem

Most AI agents never reach production. The ones that do, sit on a governed fabric.

Agent pilots stall at three predictable failure modes. Each one is an integration-and-governance problem, not an AI problem.

Failure 01

No registry of agents in the enterprise.

Teams build pilots in isolation. Six months later, no one knows which agents are running, what data they access, or who owns them.

Failure 02

No broker between agents and enterprise systems.

Each agent ships its own integrations. Authentication, rate limiting, and observability are inconsistent. Production load exposes every gap.

Failure 03

No governance signed off by the board.

Risk and compliance teams refuse to release agents into production without a policy framework. The pilot becomes a sandbox forever.

Frequently asked

What is MuleSoft Agent Fabric?
MuleSoft Agent Fabric is the governance and orchestration layer for production AI agents. It includes Agent Registry, Agent Broker, Flex Gateway, and Agent Visualizer, and enables enterprises to deploy, monitor, and govern AI agents at scale on the MuleSoft Anypoint platform.
How long does an Agent Fabric Builder engagement take?
Engagements run 6 to 12 weeks, scoped to the number of pilot agents and the integration depth. The timeline and fixed fee are agreed at assessment before any work begins.
Do we need to be on MuleSoft already?
Yes. Agent Fabric runs on MuleSoft Anypoint. If you are not on MuleSoft yet, Migration Sprint MuleSoft is typically engaged first, and Agent Fabric Builder follows in the next quarter.
What kind of agents can be built in the pilot?
Customer service triage, order management, document processing, knowledge retrieval, and pre-built industry agents for manufacturing and retail. Custom agents are scoped at assessment.
How is governance handled?
Policy framework, data classification, identity binding, and audit trail are designed in week one with your risk and compliance teams. The framework is in production by week six.
Is the engagement truly fixed-fee?
Yes. Every engagement is fixed-fee with defined scope and milestone billing. Time and materials applies only to scope changes signed in writing.
