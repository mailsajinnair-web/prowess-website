Novelis runs one of the largest aluminum rolling and recycling operations in the world. Its integration estate carried fifteen years of point-to-point interfaces between ERP, plant systems, suppliers, and customer-facing applications. The board wanted a single integration layer the business could build on for the next decade.

The problem

The integration layer Novelis inherited could not carry the next ten years.

The legacy iPaaS had become a tax on every new project. Each new interface meant another piece of brittle middleware, another point of failure, another team that had to be brought in to debug it. Development timelines on new business capabilities had stretched past the point the business could accept.

The Novelis architecture team knew where they wanted to land. MuleSoft Anypoint Platform, an API-led composable architecture, a clean catalog of reusable assets. The question was how to get there without putting production at risk across a global plant footprint.

What we built

What we built.

We started with the X2M Accelerator. Inventory of every interface in the legacy estate, classified by complexity, business criticality, and reuse potential. The accelerator produced a migration plan Novelis could defend to the steering committee in week three.

We then built the target architecture as a layered API design — system, process, and experience APIs — with a reusable asset library so each new project drew from what was already there rather than starting from scratch. The migration ran in parallel waves so the legacy estate kept running while the MuleSoft platform took on traffic.

Twinkle Khanna Rajendran's team owned the architecture. Prowess architects sat alongside them through design, build, and cutover.

For the business

What changed for the business.

The CIO's office got something it had not had in years — a credible integration roadmap with named timelines. The CFO got a maintenance bill that finally started moving in the right direction. The plant teams got integrations that did not break every six weeks.

Novelis now uses the platform as the data foundation for everything that gets built on top, including the AI use cases the business is starting to scope.
