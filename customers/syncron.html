Syncron builds the platform global manufacturers use to run their aftermarket service business — service parts, pricing, warranty, uptime. Sandeep Patil runs Expert Services, the group inside Syncron that delivers customer implementations of the platform.

How the work is structured

How the work is structured.

Syncron Expert Services owns the customer-facing implementation of the Syncron platform. Integration is a load-bearing part of every one of those implementations — the platform has to talk to its customer's ERP, CRM, warranty system, and parts catalog.

Prowess delivers integration capacity into Expert Services. Senior architects and developers working under Syncron's standards, on Syncron's customers, on a continuous basis. The engagement model is sustained delivery, not project-by-project staffing.

Four years in, Sandeep wrote the testimonial on his own initiative. The words commitment, diligence, and efficiency are his.
