Engagement delivered via Parabola9 partnership

Rich Products is one of the largest privately held food manufacturers in North America. The Prowess engagement was an AI agent build delivered through Parabola9, the AI-engineering partner Prowess works with on agent-specific implementations.

The problem

An AI use case the business cared about, with a data and integration layer that had to support it.

The business unit had a specific operational use case in mind — the kind of AI agent work that only delivers value when the data feeding it is clean and the systems the agent has to reach are wired correctly. The specific use case is held in the reference call until publication consent permits.

Production AI requires the integration and data layer underneath the agent to be in place before launch. That is where the engagement was scoped.

How it was structured

How the engagement was structured.

Parabola9 led the agent design and the model-level work. Prowess delivered the integration, the data foundation, and the agent governance. Together the engagement was scoped, delivered, and signed off without the rework cycles that stall most AI proofs of concept.

The model and the integration shipped together. Neither side was waiting for the other.

Specific architecture, scope, and outcomes are held in the reference call until publication consent permits.

Why partner-led delivery worked

Why the partner-led delivery worked.

AI agent work is two specialties at once. Agent design and model-level engineering on one side. Integration and data-foundation work on the other.

Parabola9 specializes in the model and agent side. Prowess specializes in the integration and data side. The partnership separates the two without slowing either down. Each firm sends its strongest people to the work that firm is strongest at.

Rich Products got both, in an engagement that delivered.

Outcome

The outcome.

A production AI agent shipped at Rich Products, delivered jointly with Parabola9. A reference architecture Prowess and Parabola9 now apply to comparable AI agent engagements.

The integration and data layer were treated as part of the engagement from day one, which is why the agent reached production rather than stalling at pilot.

Quantified outcomes, model-level metrics, and customer reference rights are available on request under NDA.
