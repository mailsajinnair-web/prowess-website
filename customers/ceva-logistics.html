Ongoing partnership inside CEVA's Integration COE

CEVA Logistics is one of the world's largest third-party logistics providers. Its integration platform sits behind customer onboarding, shipment visibility, warehouse orchestration, and the back-office systems that make a global 3PL run. Pratap Pillai runs the team that owns it.

How the partnership works

How the partnership works.

CEVA runs its own Integration Centre of Excellence. Pratap's team owns architecture, standards, and the platform roadmap. Prowess delivers implementation capacity into that COE — sometimes whole project teams, sometimes named senior architects who plug into CEVA's design reviews.

The work spans API platform delivery across regions, customer-onboarding integrations, and the integration patterns CEVA reuses across new business wins. Prowess architects sit inside the COE alongside Pratap's team.

The structural choice — embedded delivery, sustained over years — sits behind the word diligent in Pratap's testimonial.
