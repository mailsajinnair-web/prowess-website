Ongoing partnership

Keystone Logistics runs omnichannel fulfillment for retailers most US shoppers buy from every week. Every retailer they onboard means a new set of EDI flows, a new warehouse management touchpoint, and a new set of integration patterns that have to be right on day one of peak season.

How the work is structured

How the work is structured.

Keystone's business is winning retailer programs and standing them up fast. Every new retailer brings its own EDI standard, its own warehouse management requirements, and its own SLAs. The integration layer is where the program goes live or stalls.

Prowess runs the integration delivery alongside Ravi Joshi's team. Retailer onboarding integrations, EDI build and operate, warehouse orchestration interfaces. We sit inside the cadence of every new retailer ramp.

Three years in, the work has produced a repeatable retailer-onboarding integration pattern. New retailers ramp faster because the pattern is already there.

EDI build and operate.

Outcome

The outcome.

Three years of retailer onboarding without missing a major peak season. A reusable integration pattern Keystone now applies to every new retailer win. A named customer testimonial from Ravi Joshi on the public site.
