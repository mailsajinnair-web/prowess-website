Architect-led delivery

Architect-led delivery.

The senior architects who scope an engagement remain on it through delivery, supported by a bench of 350 certified architects. The model has held for ten years and now supports 175 customers across 25 industries.

Production AI is fundamentally an integration and data problem. Data must be trustworthy, systems must be reachable, and governance must be defensible. Prowess grew up through TIBCO and webMethods, scaled through MuleSoft, added an Informatica practice as master data became foundational, and built Salesforce Agentforce and MuleSoft Agent Fabric capabilities as production AI moved into the enterprise.

Customers describe the engagement in consistent terms. Pratap Pillai at CEVA Logistics and Sandeep Patil at Syncron each chose the word diligent in their public testimonials.

How we work

Four things to know about how we work.

Architects on the call before the contract.

The architect who scopes the engagement is the architect who designs and delivers it. A scoping call is a working session with the engineer who will be on the design review three months later.

Ten years independent.

Aravind and Ajay Konda founded Prowess in 2016, and the original leadership team continues to run the firm. Long-tenured customers retain the same architectural point of contact across program phases.

Integration and data at the core.

The practice grew up through TIBCO and webMethods, scaled through MuleSoft, expanded into Informatica as master-data foundations became central to enterprise programs, and added Salesforce Agentforce and MuleSoft Agent Fabric as production AI matured. The integration and data layer is the practice's foundation, and every adjacent capability is engineered on top of it.

Named customer references.

Twinkle Khanna Rajendran at Novelis, Pratap Pillai at CEVA Logistics, Sandeep Patil at Syncron, and Ravi Joshi at Keystone Logistics have provided public testimonials, and are available for peer reference conversations on request.

Where Prowess fits best

Where Prowess fits best.

Prowess is the right partner when integration and data are foundational to the program. Typical engagements include legacy iPaaS migration, master-data foundations engineered for AI, Agent Fabric standup across multiple enterprise systems, and API platforms supported by an operating Centre of Excellence. The four EnterpriseFLOW accelerators are purpose-built for exactly this work.
