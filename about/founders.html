Aravind Konda

Aravind Konda — Chief Executive Officer.

Aravind began his career as an enterprise integration architect at TIBCO in the early 2000s, with webMethods-era B2B and ESB design programs for global manufacturers and financial services firms. He co-founded Prowess with Ajay in 2016. As CEO he is accountable for the firm's customer portfolio, the delivery operating model, and the strategic partnerships with MuleSoft, Informatica, and Salesforce.

Aravind remains personally engaged in customer architecture conversations, and takes scoping calls with CIOs at $2B-revenue enterprises as a matter of practice.

Why we built Prowess

Why we built Prowess.

We founded Prowess in 2016 after fifteen years inside enterprise integration platforms. The work that consistently mattered to our customers was the architecture connecting strategy to code: governance, master data, reuse, and the integration layer that holds them together. That is the firm we set out to build.

Our model is straightforward. Certified architects design the engagement and remain accountable through delivery. A Centre of Excellence operating inside the firm ensures the patterns developed on one customer program inform the architecture of the next.

Ten years in, MuleSoft has recognized Prowess as Above & Beyond Partner of the Year for the Americas in both 2024 and 2025. CEVA Logistics, Syncron, Novelis, and Keystone Logistics have spoken about the work in public testimonials.

— Aravind and Ajay
